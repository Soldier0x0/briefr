BRIEFR is your self-hosted CVE intelligence platform for security analysts.

Start every day on the BRIEF tab. Your morning action queue highlights what changed overnight, with live stats and a publication heatmap.

The FEED tab is your prioritized CVE stream, filtered to your stack. Click any card to open rich detail.

The drawer shows operational priority, threat score, exploitation signals, and remediation guidance — all explainable.

IOC LOOKUP enriches IPs, hashes, and domains across threat intelligence providers.

FORGE connects intel to detection — map ATT&CK techniques, build hunt packs, and generate Sigma rules.

Self-hosted. Open source. Try the live demo at briefrdemo dot projectjupiter dot in.
