What it does

Q: What does `backend/intel/provenance.py` do?

A: Per-section intel provenance (FR1 — review §5.4).

Q: What does `backend/ioc/retro_match.py` do?

A: Local IOC watchlist retro-match (V1.5 Theme 4b). Copyright © 2026 Sai Harsha Vardhan SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Q: What does `backend/jobs/app.py` do?

A: Procrastinate App factory. No-op unless PROCRASTINATE_ENABLED=1 and Postgres.

Q: What does `backend/jobs/context.py` do?

A: Outbound attribution contextvars for metering (Q2) and job wrappers (Q1).

Q: What does `backend/jobs/retry_policy.py` do?

A: Bounded retry policy for durable Procrastinate tasks.

Q: What does `backend/jobs/tasks.py` do?

A: Procrastinate task definitions (Blueprint — bound at App creation).

Q: What does `backend/jobs/worker.py` do?

A: In-process Procrastinate worker lifecycle (single-box default).

Q: What does `backend/metrics/request_counter.py` do?

A: In-process HTTP request counter — read-and-reset by the resource collector.

Q: What does `backend/migration/sqlite_to_postgres.py` do?

A: One-shot SQLite -> PostgreSQL data migration, driven from the admin panel. Reuses Alembic for DDL on the target and copies rows from the on-disk SQLite file. Setting DATABASE_URL (via Admin -> Database) switches the running app to PostgreSQL; this module never writes to briefr.db after that point. Copyright © 2026 Sai Harsha Vardhan SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Q: What does `backend/preferences/display_validate.py` do?

A: Validation for per-user display preferences and timezone.

Q: What does `backend/preferences/repo.py` do?

A: DB access for per-user stack terms and optional asset profile.

Q: What does `backend/preferences/validate.py` do?

A: Validation for per-user stack terms and asset profile JSON.

Q: What does `backend/proof/bench.py` do?

A: File-based rule proof bench (V1.5 Theme 2). Copyright © 2026 Sai Harsha Vardhan SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0

Q: What does `backend/scripts/backfill_poc.py` do?

A: One-shot backfill of has_poc from stored source_urls. Safe to run while backend is up.

Q: What does `backend/scripts/create_user.py` do?

A: Create or reset a built-in app login account. Never exposed over HTTP — this is the only way accounts get provisioned (decision 2026-06-11). Usage: python3 scripts/create_user.py --username ops python3 scripts/create_user.py --username ops --password 'hunter2' --non-interactive

Q: What does `backend/scripts/delete_user.py` do?

A: Erase a built-in app login account and all data tied to it (sessions, preferences, IOC watchlist, notifications). Never exposed over HTTP — user lifecycle management is CLI-only by design (see scripts/create_user.py). This is the operational counterpart to a data-subject erasure request: an operator can run this to honor a "delete my account" request without a manual SQL query. Usage: python3 scripts/delete_user.py --username ops python3 scripts/

Q: What does `backend/scripts/sync_env.py` do?

A: Sync .env with .env.example: keep existing values, add missing keys. Walks .env.example line by line (preserving its comments/section grouping and blank-line spacing) and regenerates .env, substituting in any value the user already set. Keys present in .env but not in .env.example are appended under a trailing "Custom / not in .env.example" section so nothing is lost.

Q: What does `backend/security_architecture/corpus_drift.py` do?

A: PM-3d: on-demand security architecture corpus drift check.

Q: What does `backend/security_architecture/corpus_loader.py` do?

A: TM-1: load + validate the Security Architecture Corpus (SAC). Schema rules (spec §4.1): every entity has id/title/summary/status/owner/ origin; curated entities additionally carry review_date/evidence[]/ related_ids[]. `related_ids[]` must resolve to real ids elsewhere in the corpus -- a dangling reference is a validation error, not a warning, so the corpus can't silently rot into referencing deleted entities.
