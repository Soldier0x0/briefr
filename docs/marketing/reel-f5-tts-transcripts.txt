How does BRIEFR decide what matters?

[00:10.0 – 00:28.0]  RISK WEIGHTS
Every CVE gets a risk score from zero to one hundred. Six weighted factors. Asset match against your stack is the largest at thirty-five percent. Then CISA KEV listing at twenty-five, EPSS probability at fifteen, exploit availability, CVSS, and momentum. Same CVE, same stack, same score. Every time.

[00:28.0 – 00:44.0]  STACK MATCHING
Stack matching is term-based. Vendor and product strings from your profile against affected products in each CVE. Matches are labeled so you can judge them.

[00:44.0 – 01:02.0]  CORRELATION
The correlation engine links CVEs that share OTX indicators, infrastructure, or campaigns. So you see clusters, not isolated tickets.

[01:02.0 – 01:18.0]  EMBEDDINGS
For semantic search, BRIEFR uses BAAI bge-small-en-v1.5 embeddings on CPU through fastembed. No GPU required. Find related CVEs by meaning, not just keyword overlap.

[01:18.0 – 01:30.0]  CTA
Scoring and correlation run on a fixed schedule. Your browser reads cached results. Fast triage, reproducible intelligence. github.com/Soldier0x0/briefr

Where does BRIEFR data come from, and where does it live?

[00:10.0 – 00:32.0]  FEEDS
A background scheduler ingests NVD, CISA KEV, EPSS, MITRE ATT and CK, AlienVault OTX, ExploitDB, OSV, SigmaHQ, and more. Public feeds are free. IOC enrichment uses optional free-tier API keys for VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB, and GreyNoise.

[00:32.0 – 00:52.0]  POSTGRES
Everything lands in one PostgreSQL sixteen database. Intel tables and app tables in separate schemas. The scheduler writes. The API reads. No microservices, no separate message queue. Production uses the pgvector image for embeddings and the SigmaHQ rule index.

[00:52.0 – 01:10.0]  DEPLOY
Docker compose up, set your database URL, and you have vector-enabled Postgres. Minimum hardware: two vCPU, two gigabytes for local SQLite development. Recommended: two vCPU, four gigabytes with PostgreSQL for a small team deployment.

[01:10.0 – 01:30.0]  CTA
One deployment. Your data stays on your server. Apache two point oh. No vendor lock-in. github.com/Soldier0x0/briefr
